FAQ

Septic Questions East Texas Homeowners Ask Us

Straight answers to what we get asked on the phone every week. Still stuck? Call +1 936-208-3153 and ask us directly.

How much does a new septic system cost in Lufkin or Jasper?

Cost comes down to tank size, whether you need a conventional or aerobic system, how much drainfield your soil requires, and county permit fees — which is why an honest number needs an on-site look. The estimate is free and the price we give you is flat-rate and in writing. We break down what actually moves the number in our guide to septic system costs in East Texas.

How often should I pump my septic tank?

For most East Texas households, every three to five years. Larger families, garbage disposals and undersized tanks push that closer to every two or three. Skipping it is what turns a $400 pump-out into a drainfield replacement. Here is how to work out the right interval for your household.

What are the warning signs that my septic system is failing?

Slow drains across the whole house, gurgling pipes, sewage smell near the tank or field, standing water or unusually green grass over the drainfield, and backups in the lowest fixtures first. Any one of these is worth a call now rather than next month — see all seven warning signs of a failing septic system.

Do I need a permit to install a septic system in Angelina County?

Yes. Texas requires a permit and a site evaluation for any new on-site sewage facility, and Angelina County runs its own review and inspection process. We handle the paperwork, the soil evaluation and the inspection scheduling as part of the install. The full process is in our Angelina County septic permit guide.

Do you offer emergency septic service?

Yes — 24 hours a day, seven days a week. If your system is backing up, overflowing or pooling in the yard, call +1 936-208-3153 and we will get a technician moving toward you, usually the same day.

How soon can you get out to my property?

Most non-emergency jobs across Lufkin, Jasper and the surrounding counties are scheduled within the same week. Emergencies are handled the day you call, including weekends.

Should I install an aerobic or a conventional septic system?

It depends on lot size, soil percolation and how much drainfield you can fit. Sandy East Texas soil often supports a conventional system; clay-heavy ground or a tight lot usually needs aerobic treatment. We compare both, with real costs and maintenance loads, in aerobic vs. conventional septic systems.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free and there is no obligation. You get a flat-rate price in writing before any work starts, so nothing on the final invoice is a surprise.
