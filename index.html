How do I know which service I need?+

Tell us about the symptoms or your property plans. We can help you decide whether to start with an inspection, repair, maintenance visit, or an installation assessment.

How much will the work cost?+

Every property is different. System type, soil, access, and the scope of work affect the price. We provide free estimates and a clear quote before work begins.

Can you help with a real estate inspection?+

Yes. Our septic inspections help buyers and sellers understand the condition of a property’s system. Let us know your timeline when you request a visit.

What if I have a septic emergency?+

Call (936) 208–3153 directly for emergency help. Avoid using water if sewage is backing up, and keep people and pets away from affected areas.

Is an online booking immediately confirmed?+

An online booking is a request for your preferred date and arrival window. We’ll call or text to confirm availability and discuss your needs before an appointment is finalized.
