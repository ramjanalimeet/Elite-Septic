Replacement Overview

When Septic System Replacement Is the Honest Answer

A field that surfaces for months every winter, a tank leaking groundwater in after every rain, an RV park system that backs up on every busy weekend, or a kitchen whose grease has ruined the field: these are the cases where a repair only delays the bill. We tell you when you have reached that point, and we show you the evidence.

When a repair will hold, that is what we recommend. Replacing a system costs far more, and we would rather you spend that money only when it is the lasting answer.

Replacing a system that the water defeated

If the old system failed because the seasonal water table rose into the field, putting a new conventional field in the same place will fail the same way. The replacement has to change something: a higher site on the property, a design that needs less unsaturated soil, or treatment before dispersal.

On many lake and bottomland lots, that means an aerobic unit with spray or drip dispersal. In Trinity County, those require an Affidavit to the Public recorded with the County Clerk, and the county holds the final permit until it receives a certified copy. We take care of that as part of the job.

Step By Step

Our Septic System Replacement Process in Trinity County

Texas rules set the order, and Trinity County adds its authorization and affidavit steps.

1. Find Out Why It Failed

Water, overload, grease or age. Knowing the cause decides what the replacement must do differently.

2. Evaluate the Site

A licensed evaluator records soil texture and the depth of the seasonal water table across possible sites.

3. Design and Apply

A licensed professional designs the system, and we return the application to the County Judge's office.

4. Authorization to Construct

Once the county approves, it issues the Authorization to Construct. Work does not start before we have it.

5. Build and Retire the Old System

We install the new system, inspect before cover and decommission the old tank.

6. Affidavit and Handover

For aerobic systems, the affidavit is recorded with the County Clerk, the final permit released and the maintenance contract set up.

Can a home or business keep operating during a septic replacement?

Usually, yes. While the application is under review, we can pump the old system to keep it usable. For homes, the final connection typically means a short break from water use, often part of a day. For RV parks, campgrounds and restaurants, we plan the work around your quieter days and can phase it so part of the property stays open. Tell us your busiest dates when we quote, and we will schedule around them.

Like for Like or Upgrade

Replacing Like for Like vs. Changing the System

Near water, a like-for-like replacement often repeats the original failure. These are the deciding factors.

When the same type of system makes sense

  • The site is high and well above the water table
  • The old system failed from age, not water
  • Use has not changed since it was installed
  • There is room for a field in fresh soil
  • The property serves a single household
  • No grease or chemical load is involved
  • A conventional system meets current rules

When the replacement septic system should be different

  • The old field drowned in winter
  • The site is near the lake or a creek
  • The business has grown beyond the design
  • A kitchen sends heavy grease to the system
  • RV tank chemicals are part of the load
  • Usable soil is thin above the water table
  • There is no higher ground available

Septic System Replacement FAQs for Trinity County, TX

Common questions from Trinity County homes and businesses facing a replacement.

My field floods every winter. Will a new field fix it?

Not in the same spot with the same design. The replacement has to use higher ground, need less unsaturated soil, or treat effluent first. Near the lake that usually means an aerobic system.

What does the County Judge's office handle?

Trinity County administers septic permitting through the County Judge’s office in Groveton, including the review and the Authorization to Construct. We prepare and submit the application.

What is the aerobic affidavit?

An Affidavit to the Public that must be notarized and recorded with the County Clerk for aerobic systems. The county holds the final permit until it has a certified copy.

Can my RV park stay open during a replacement?

Often partly. We phase work around your bookings and keep the old system pumped where we can.

Will a restaurant replacement be different from a home?

Yes. It is sized on use, permitted in the commercial category and designed with grease in mind.

What happens to the old tank?

It is pumped and properly decommissioned so it cannot collapse or hold water.

How long does the process take?

Allow for the evaluation, design and county review, which can take up to 30 days, plus the installation and, for aerobic systems, the affidavit recording.

How much does a replacement cost in Trinity County?

It depends on the water table, the soil, the system type and the use. We quote a flat rate after the evaluation.

Do you charge extra for travel?

No. The price is the same anywhere in Trinity County.
