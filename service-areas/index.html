FAQ

Service Area Questions We Get Asked

Coverage, travel charges and response times — the things people ring up to check before they book. Still unsure? Call +1 936-208-3153.

Do you serve my town if it is not listed on this page?

Most likely yes. The towns named here are the main ones, but we cover the whole of Angelina, Polk, Rusk, Houston, Trinity and Sabine counties plus Jasper — including plenty of places too small to list. Call +1 936-208-3153, read out your road, and we will tell you straight away.

Do you charge extra to travel to rural properties?

No. There is no distance surcharge inside our service area. The flat-rate price we quote is the price whether you are in town in Lufkin or well out in Sabine County.

How quickly can you get to me in an emergency?

Emergencies are handled the day you call, 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Non-emergency work across the service area is usually scheduled inside the same week.

Does the type of septic system I need depend on which county I am in?

Very much so. Sandy ground in Rusk County often drains well enough for a conventional system, while small lots and a high water table near Lake Livingston in Polk County frequently call for aerobic treatment instead. Around Lufkin the claypan makes that decision for you. We evaluate the soil on your lot before quoting — see aerobic vs. conventional systems.

Who handles the septic permit in my county?

We do. Each county runs its own review and inspection process for new on-site sewage facilities, and we deal with the paperwork and scheduling as part of the install. Our Angelina County permit guide walks through what the process looks like.

Do you offer free estimates everywhere you work?

Yes — estimates are free and carry no obligation anywhere in the service area, and you get a flat-rate price in writing before any work starts.
